# Intro comic - script and image prompts

Two pages, four panels each. Everyone is nameless for now.

**The story.** The humans are gone, or nearly - a rare few at most. The machines inherited the
world. The techno-lords rule the neon city behind its walls; everything that didn't make the
cut was left outside, in the **Scrapheap**.

He is a mechanic robot - an **older model**, not as capable as the machines of the city. She is
his **robot friend**. She carries an **old chip** with code the city needs. The lords' Collectors
(armoured hunter cars) take her - and keep her **intact**, because they don't know whether they
can get the code out without breaking her. He builds a war-car from scrap and drives the one
road into the city to get her back.

**Why the hunters lose interest when you get out and walk.** They mostly track the CAR - new
technology, and that's what they've evolved to hunt. He is an old model, vastly ignored by the
city's guards, who only fear high-tech androids packed with powerful energy attacks.

**How it maps onto the game.** Leg 1: leaving the Scrapheap, the fleet on the road. The bend:
the point of no return. Leg 2: the edge of the city closing in. The city: out of the car he is
beneath their notice. The enemy colours (blue, green, yellow) are the Collectors' livery.

**Text.** Keep ALL lettering out of the images (models garble it). The captions below are
typed over the panels by the game, in our font, so the wording can change any time.

---

## Shared blocks - paste these into every prompt, word for word

**STYLE:** 1980s American comic book panel. Bold black ink outlines, flat colours with halftone
dot shading, dramatic shadows. Limited palette: deep violet and near-black shadows, hot magenta
(#ff2bd6) and electric cyan (#22f0ff) neon light, rust-orange accents. No text, no speech
bubbles, no captions, no lettering, no signatures anywhere in the image. Landscape 3:2.

**HIM:** a tall, lean, outdated-looking robot: a dented gunmetal chest plate, one arm of rusted
orange metal and one of newer chrome, a round head with a single cracked cyan optic lens, a
welding visor flipped up on his head, a worn leather apron with oil stains.

**HER:** a slender robot, sleeker than him, with smooth pale plating, two round cyan optic eyes
and a small square socket on her chest where an old data chip glows faintly cyan.

**COLLECTORS:** low armoured hunter cars with blue, green and yellow plating, no windows, a bar
of harsh white searchlights where headlights would be, folded mechanical grabbing arms.

**WAR-CAR:** a red-orange muscle car rebuilt from scrap, welded armour plates, mismatched wheels,
a small cannon turret on the roof.

## Page 1 - the loss

**1. The world** - caption: *"The humans are gone. They left us the world, and nothing to do in it."*
> [STYLE] A wide establishing shot at dusk. In the far distance, a neon city of impossibly tall
> towers glowing magenta and cyan behind a massive wall. In the foreground, an endless junkyard
> of scrapped machines, broken robots and wrecked cars piled into hills, with small lights of
> shacks built from scrap. A single highway runs from the junkyard toward the city. Moody,
> lonely, epic.

**2. The workshop** - caption: *"Out here, we had nothing they wanted. Almost."*
> [STYLE] Inside a cramped workshop made of scrap, lit by a hanging lamp and the glow of a
> welding torch. [HIM] works at a bench, sparks flying. [HER] sits on a crate beside the bench,
> handing him a tool. Warm, quiet moment.

**3. The raid** - no caption
> [STYLE] Night. The scrapyard outside the workshop is flooded with harsh white searchlights.
> Three [COLLECTORS] have surrounded it; one has extended its mechanical arm and is lifting
> [HER] off the ground, carefully, while a scanner beam lights up the glowing chip in her chest.
> [HIM] is thrown back in the foreground. Chaos, motion lines, strong diagonal composition.

**4. Gone** - caption: *"They didn't want her. They wanted what she carries."*
> [STYLE] [HIM] on his knees in the dirt of the empty, wrecked scrapyard, seen from behind. In
> the far distance, the red tail lights of the [COLLECTORS] heading down the highway toward the
> glowing neon city. Quiet, heavy.

## Page 2 - the road

**5. The build** - no caption
> [STYLE] A montage panel in the workshop: [HIM] with the welding visor down, building the
> [WAR-CAR] from scrap, showers of sparks, armour plates being welded on, the roof turret being
> bolted in place. Determined, intense, glowing sparks.

**6. The road** - caption: *"One road in. Every hunter they own on it."*
> [STYLE] Dawn. The [WAR-CAR] races along a straight highway away from the junkyard, dust
> behind it, toward the distant neon city. Behind and around it, several [COLLECTORS] are
> closing in. Seen from a low angle at the roadside, speed lines, high action.

**7. The gate** - caption: *"They hunt only new tech. An old model like me? Beneath their notice."*
> [STYLE] The highway reaching the city: a giant gate in the wall, towers rising beyond it, armed
> guard robots and drones sweeping white searchlight beams across the road - every beam locked
> on vehicles, none on the ground around them. The [WAR-CAR] small in the frame, about to cross
> into the city. Ominous scale.

**8. The city** - caption: *"Hold on. I'm coming."*
> [STYLE] A full splash panel. [HIM] on foot in a narrow neon street of the city, seen from
> behind and slightly below, looking up at towers covered in magenta and cyan signs. The parked
> [WAR-CAR] behind him. Rain, reflections, glowing haze. Lonely and resolute.

## Tips

- Generate each panel on its own (they become the game's intro screens one by one); assemble
  pages later if you want them printed.
- If HIM or HER drift between panels, start the next prompt with: "Same robot design as the
  previous image:" and paste the character block again.
- Keep the 3:2 landscape so the panels fill the 16:9 screen with little cropping.
